I'm proud to share that our team, **Timrad** from **Khatam University**, won **1st place in the RoboCup Rescue Simulation League, Agent Competition**, at **RoboCup 2025** in **Salvador, Brazil** (15–27 July 2025).

![1st place, RoboCup 2025 Rescue Simulation League Agent Competition: Team Timrad, Khatam University](/images/blog/robocup-2025-first-place-timrad.jpg)
*1st Place: Team Timrad, Khatam University, RoboCup 2025 Salvador*

The Rescue Agent Simulation League challenges teams to build **autonomous agents**, such as fire brigades, police forces and ambulance teams, that coordinate their response to a simulated disaster in a city. Agents have to make decisions with limited information and communication, which makes it a demanding test of **multi-agent coordination, planning and decision-making under uncertainty**.

**Team Timrad:** M. A. Ghaemi, H. Alizadegan and M. Asghari Ilani.

Thank you to my teammates, and congratulations to all the teams who took part.
