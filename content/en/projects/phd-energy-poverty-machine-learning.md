My PhD at **Swansea University**, on the **WGSSS Doctoral Training Partnership** (Management and Business pathway, funded by ESRC/UKRI), asks how machine learning can help identify **energy poverty** and **warn households before they become fuel vulnerable**.

Work so far includes:

- **Household, regional and climate information in identifying energy poverty**: machine-learning evidence from five European countries (*Journal of Environmental Management*, 2026).
- **Too Little, Too Late? Early warning of fuel vulnerability risk for UK households** (*Risk Analysis*, 2026, accepted).
