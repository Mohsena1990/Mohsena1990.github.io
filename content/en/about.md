intro: >-
  **Data scientist and researcher** working on **machine learning for socio-economic, energy and environmental risk**. I am a **PhD researcher** at <a href="https://www.swansea.ac.uk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Swansea University</strong></a>, funded by **ESRC/UKRI** through the **Wales Graduate School for the Social Sciences (WGSSS)** Doctoral Training Partnership, on the **Management and Business pathway**. My research asks practical questions with real consequences, such as *what really drives energy poverty?* and *can we warn households before it is too late?*, and answers them with predictive models that are rigorous, interpretable and useful for decision-making.

study: >-
  I started out as a **mechanical engineer**, with a BSc and an MSc from the <a href="https://ut.ac.ir/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">University of Tehran</a>, followed by three years in R&D at **Iran Khodro**. Since 2019 I have worked as an independent researcher in **machine learning**, and from 2024 to 2026 I was a **Senior Data Scientist at Dotin**, building and deploying models for real-world applications. At Swansea I combine that background with social research training, including an **MSc in Social Research with Distinction**.

passion_title: "What I focus on"
passion_text: >-
  I build **forecasting and early-warning models for risk**: **energy poverty** across five European countries, household **fuel vulnerability** in the UK, **market price** forecasting, **greenhouse-gas emissions** and electricity-grid signals, **groundwater irrigation risk** under salinity–sodicity stress, and **energy consumption** prediction. I also work on **computer vision and NLP**, including MRI-based brain tumour classification and SAR flood segmentation. My work has appeared in *Risk Analysis*, *Journal of Environmental Management*, *Energy and AI*, *Scientific Reports* and *IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters*.

mix: >-
  An engineering background taught me to care about **how models behave under stress**, not just about headline accuracy. I like to audit apparent machine-learning gains carefully, combine tabular, spatial and time-series data, and translate results into findings that policymakers and practitioners can act on. I work mainly in **Python** (PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, Pandas), as well as **R**, **MATLAB** and **SQL**.

personal: >-
  I live in **Cardiff**. In 2025 I was part of the team that won **1st place in the RoboCup 2025 Rescue Agent Simulation League** in Brazil, building agents that coordinate disaster-response simulations.
