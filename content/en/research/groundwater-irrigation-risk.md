**Authors:** **M. Asghari Ilani**, S. Mokhtar Tehran, A. Kavei, M. Banad, H. Eskandari

**Published in:** *Journal of Environmental Management (2026)*

Full title: *Forecasting Groundwater Irrigation Risk under Salinity–Sodicity Stress Using Hydrochemical–Climate Data Fusion: A Tabular, Spatial, and Ordinal Learning Framework for Environmental Management.* The paper fuses **hydrochemical and climate data** and combines **tabular, spatial and ordinal learning** to forecast the risk of using groundwater for irrigation under salinity and sodicity stress.
